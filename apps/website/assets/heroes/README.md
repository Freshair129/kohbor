# Hero portraits

The website shows these six portraits as hologram cards walking down the lanes (`HEROES` in `js/stage.js`): juggernaut, nevermore, crystal_maiden, lina, sven and pudge.

- They are byte-for-byte copies of `apps/desktop/src/assets/dota/6993/heroes/<id>.png` in this repository, exported from the owner's installed Dota 2 (build 6993), read only, with no download. Their SHA-256 values are in `apps/desktop/src/assets/dota/6993/card-provenance.json`. On 2026-11-25 the copies were refreshed from 6993 after the YCoCg colour fix; the earlier 6989 copies of crystal_maiden, juggernaut, pudge and sven had wrong colours.
- They are Valve game art. The owner asked for hero holograms on the website on 2026-11-23. The rights decision for public use is the owner's.
