Design proposal. Not approved (DEC-VISUALS open). Sample data only. Nothing here replaces application code. Identity tokens (A1, E1, names) and the minimap picture are neutral placeholders, not game artwork.

The Command Deck as a second monitor: the live minimap in the middle, your team on the left, the enemy team and recent events on the right. Read it from the corner of the eye; nothing needs the mouse during a match.

Mockups

A · Deck Live1920 × 1080, 2560 × 1440, 1366 × 768, 1024 × 768 and portrait 1080 × 1920; state, mode and scope stepping.B · Minimap panelLive frame, rate and age badge, the admitted marker set, selection, critical, and six degraded states.C · CardsOwn hero; allies in every scope combination; stale, dead, unknown; enemy slots.D + G · Social and flowSigned-out state, sign-in, friends and presence, the Share list with both-direction scopes, the first-time sheet, pause and remove, “why is this friend not receiving”, and the flow diagram.E · Events and inspectorDetected versus heard history rows for all fourteen outcomes, the inspector drawer, the audio dock slot.F · Glance, idle, themesGlance versus detail, no-game and menu states, dark and light, burn-in approach.

DESIGN-NOTES.md holds the decisions, state tables, contrast table, type scale, motion spec, differences from the brief and from the first proposal, open decisions and the list of what was not verified. tokens.css holds the tokens and the shared component styles.
