History row: detected is not the same as heard

Each row has two lines. Line one is what was detected. Line two is the audio outcome (EVENT-AUDIO-SPEC §5) with one of three glyphs: filled = heard, half = started then cut, slashed = not heard, always followed by the reason in words.

Inspector drawer

  • Opens over the event region only. The map box, both team columns and the audio slot stay where they are (UI-002: map within 1 CSS px).
  • Starts closed. Live refresh never opens it or moves focus. Enter or Space on a focused card or a known marker opens it; Esc closes inline detail first, then the drawer; focus returns to the card. Not a modal, no focus trap.
  • Entitlement only changes how much the drawer shows. Telemetry, alerts, overlay and voice alerts never depend on it.
